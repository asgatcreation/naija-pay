A Spring Data page, which is what every Monnify list endpoint returns: content, pageable,
totalElements, last. Pages are zero-based.

THE SPEC IS WRONG ABOUT THIS ENDPOINT, in two ways, and this fixture follows the live
sandbox rather than the document:

1. The spec's example shows the page at the TOP LEVEL, outside `responseBody`, and the
   adapter was built with a fallback for exactly that. The sandbox returns the ordinary
   envelope: `requestSuccessful`, `responseMessage`, `responseCode`, `responseBody`. The
   fallback is kept as a defensive measure — Monnify's own document says otherwise, so
   either shape may appear — but the enveloped one is what actually arrives.

2. The spec's item is flat: `customerName`, `customerEmail`, `transactionDate`. The real
   item nests the customer under `customerDTO`, dates the row with `createdOn` (the
   zoneless `"2024-11-25 07:35:17.566"` shape), names the currency `currencyCode`, and
   carries `paymentMethodList` and `collectionChannel`.

Two consequences for callers, both recorded in PROVIDERS.md:

- An item from this endpoint has `amount` and NO `totalPayable` or `amountPaid`, so
  `amountPaid` and `amountSettled` are null on every row. The amounts here are numbers,
  where the verify response sends strings for the same fields.
- `channel` is null on every row. `collectionChannel` is how the payment was collected
  (a reserved account, the web SDK), not which instrument was used, and
  `paymentMethodList` on a pending row is what was ENABLED rather than what was paid
  with. The adapter reports neither as the channel, because neither is the claim.

Personal data: the customer name and email here are invented. The live response carries
real rows from Monnify's shared public test merchant.
