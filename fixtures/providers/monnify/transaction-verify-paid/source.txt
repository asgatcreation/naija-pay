The verify response, and the fixture that pins two Monnify quirks:

  amountPaid, totalPayable and settlementAmount are STRINGS ("100.00"), not numbers
  paidOn is "25/07/2022 11:20:20 AM" -- dd/MM/yyyy with a 12-hour clock and no timezone

Handing that date to a lenient parser gives either an invalid date or July read as the
25th month, so normalise.ts and normalise.py parse it explicitly and treat it as UTC.
paymentStatus "PAID" is Monnify's word for a completed collection.
