Every bank Monnify can pay. Monnify is the only Tier 1 provider that publishes USSD
templates per bank, and several banks have none -- the nulls in this fixture are real.

The codes are a mix of three-digit CBN codes (044) and five-digit microfinance codes
(50515), which is why bank matching treats the provider's code as authoritative and uses
naija-data only for enrichment.
