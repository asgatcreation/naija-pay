A created transfer when two-factor disbursement is on -- which it is by DEFAULT on a new
Monnify integration.

This is the most dangerous response in the Monnify API to misread. requestSuccessful is
true and the HTTP status is 200, and NO MONEY HAS MOVED: status is
PENDING_AUTHORIZATION, and nothing happens until an OTP from the registered email is
submitted to /api/v2/disbursements/single/validate-otp. The adapter maps the status to
pending and sets awaitingOtp, so a caller is not left waiting for a webhook that will
never arrive.

Monnify documents six transfer states: PENDING, SUCCESS, FAILED, REVERSED,
PENDING_AUTHORIZATION and OTP_EMAIL_DISPATCH_FAILED.
