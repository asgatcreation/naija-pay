A batch transfer. onValidationFailure decides what one bad row does to the batch: BREAK
abandons the whole batch and CONTINUE skips the row. The adapter defaults to BREAK,
because the safe default for money is to do nothing until a human looks.

The spec's required list for this endpoint names a `title` field that its own schema does
not define, so the adapter sends it anyway rather than risk a rejection.
