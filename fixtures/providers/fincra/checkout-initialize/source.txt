A hosted checkout. The amount is a DECIMAL in major units: 350 is three hundred and fifty
naira, where Paystack would take 35000 kobo.

Note the envelope key: this endpoint answers `status`, where the payout, bank and wallet
endpoints answer `success` for exactly the same meaning. The adapter accepts either.
