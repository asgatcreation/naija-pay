A permanent NGN virtual account. Assembled from the request page's own schema and its
response example for the by-BVN route, whose fields are identical and whose own sample is
masked with asterisks -- an unmasked example is more useful as a fixture.

BVN is required for NGN, and a corporate account additionally requires businessName and
bvnName, which Fincra matches against the name on the BVN. Both are enforced locally by
the adapter, because Fincra rejects a mismatch without saying which field was wrong.

status "approved" is the only value that means the account is usable: pending and reviewed
mean Fincra is still looking, declined means it refused, and expired and closed mean the
account is gone. isActive and isSuspended are separate flags, and an account is only
usable when both agree.
