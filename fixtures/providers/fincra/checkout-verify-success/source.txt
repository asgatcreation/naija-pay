Verifying a payment by YOUR reference -- Fincra has no verify-by-its-own-reference route
for checkout, which is the opposite of Flutterwave.

The x-business-id header is required on this call and on no other checkout call.

Fincra's documented checkout statuses are initiated, pending, success, successful, failed
and expired, and a charge left pending for more than two hours becomes expired. The
business settings that Fincra also returns on this endpoint are omitted from this
fixture: they are merchant configuration rather than payment data.
