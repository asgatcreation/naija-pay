One wallet per currency, which is what Fincra's "wallets" are: the business's own
balances, not accounts opened for customers.

Both balances here carry SIX decimal places -- 1999965363.990817 and 4145.558824. Neither
can be expressed exactly in kobo or pence, so the adapter reports them as null rather
than rounding, and the exact figures stay in `raw`. A balance that is wrong by a fraction
is worse than a balance that says "I cannot tell you this exactly", because only one of
those gets noticed.

lockedBalance is money committed to payouts that have not settled, and 172950 converts
cleanly -- so a fixture with a mix of convertible and inconvertible figures is the useful
one to keep.
