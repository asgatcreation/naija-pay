A refund, which at Fincra is always a refund of a whole collection: the refund endpoint
takes only a reference, so a PARTIAL refund is not possible through this API.

Two things this fixture pins:
  the amounts are STRINGS with six decimal places ("5914.000000"), and the trailing zeros
  mean they still convert exactly, unlike the wallet balances
  completedAt and initiatedAt are "2023-12-07 17:39:00" -- ISO with a space and no
  timezone. JavaScript reads a zoneless stamp as local time and Python returns a naive
  datetime, so both packages pin it to UTC; without that, the two would disagree about
  when this refund happened.

refundType "overpayment" is why the refund exists: the customer paid 6764 against an
expected 850.
