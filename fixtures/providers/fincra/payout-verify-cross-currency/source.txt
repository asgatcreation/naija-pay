A cross-currency payout, which is Fincra's whole proposition and the reason this fixture
is kept rather than only a naira one.

Three things it pins:
  fee is 9090.909091 -- SIX decimal places. No currency has six, so core/money refuses to
  convert it and the adapter reports fee as null rather than inventing a rounded figure.
  The exact number stays in `raw`.
  amountSent and amountReceived are in DIFFERENT currencies, so they are separate fields
  rather than one amount.
  isDocumentRequired comes back as 0 here and as false elsewhere, so both are accepted.
