Name enquiry, as a POST rather than a GET, with a required `type` that selects the kind of
identifier: nuban, bank_account, iban, mobile_money or interac_etransfer.

Fincra does not echo the bank code back on a NUBAN resolve, so the adapter carries through
the one it was given.
