Flutterwave's documented wording for an empty balance. Unlike Paystack, whose
wording is "Your balance is not enough to fulfil this request", Flutterwave says
"Insufficient funds" -- the two were found to differ by running against Paystack's
sandbox, which is why each provider's classifier matches its own wording rather
than sharing one list.
