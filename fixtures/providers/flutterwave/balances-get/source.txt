Balances are decimals in major units. 20000.5 is NGN 20,000.50 -- that is
2,000,050 kobo, and 3.29 USD is 329 cents. A naive float multiply by 100 gives
328.99999999999994 for the second one, which is why core/money converts through
fromMajor and asserts the round trip.
