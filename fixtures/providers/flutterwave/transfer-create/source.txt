Recorded from Flutterwave's documented sample response with identifiers replaced.

data.status is "NEW" on creation: the transfer is QUEUED, not completed. Treating
"status": "success" at the top level as a completed payout is the single most
dangerous misreading of this response, and the adapter maps NEW to pending.

requires_approval and is_approved are integers (0/1), not booleans.
