Recorded from Flutterwave's documented sample response, with the card number,
customer name and references replaced by fake values.

Two fields matter and are easy to confuse:
  amount          what the customer was asked for
  charged_amount  what was actually taken (can exceed amount when the customer
                  bears the fee)
  amount_settled  what reaches your balance after fees
These are decimals in major units, not kobo.
