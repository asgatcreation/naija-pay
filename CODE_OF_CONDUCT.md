# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome here, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We
pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise, and learn from them.
- Remember that contributors work across many time zones, first languages and experience levels.
  Plain, clear English helps everyone.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commits — and when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **asgatcreation@gmail.com**. Reports are reviewed promptly and
handled in confidence; the reporter's identity is not disclosed.

The maintainer may respond with a private warning, a required apology, a temporary ban from
interaction, or a permanent ban, depending on severity and whether the behaviour is repeated.
Enforcement decisions are explained to the person concerned.
